Roadside Assistance in Lancaster, Ohio

Stuck with a flat, a dead battery, an empty tank, or keys you cannot get to? We come to you.

Starting at $100 Mon to Fri, 4PM to 9PM Lancaster, OH

What is covered

Our starting rate covers the five most common roadside problems. One price, no membership, and no annual subscription to sign up for.

  • Air. Tyre inflation when you are running low or flat.
  • Battery. Jump start for a vehicle that will not turn over.
  • Gas. Fuel delivery to get you to the nearest station.
  • Lost key. Help when your key is lost or inaccessible.
  • Locked out. Entry assistance when your keys are shut inside.

Hours and coverage

We operate Monday to Friday, 4PM to 9PM, in Lancaster, Ohio. Those hours deliberately cover the evening commute and the time after most shops have closed, which is when help is hardest to find.

If you are in immediate danger, in a live traffic lane, or involved in a collision, call 911 first. We are a convenience service, not an emergency service.

How pricing works

Service starts at $100 and covers callout and labour for any of the five services above. Consumables such as delivered fuel are charged at cost on top. We confirm the total with you before we set out, so you know the price before we arrive.

What we need from you

  • Your exact location, including any cross street or landmark
  • Vehicle make, model, and colour
  • Which of the five services you need
  • Proof that the vehicle is yours, for lockout and lost key calls
