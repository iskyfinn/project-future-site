Cloud & Infrastructure

Secure, reliable cloud environments on AWS and other platforms, built to get your applications online and keep your systems running.

Starting at $300 Project based

Cloud setup and deployment

We plan and configure cloud infrastructure around your applications, budget, and business needs. Whether you are starting fresh or moving an existing workload, we help you build an environment you can understand and maintain.

  • Cloud environment setup. Account configuration, compute, storage, and services on AWS, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, and other platforms suited to your project.
  • Application deployment and migration. Get websites, web applications, and business workloads online, or move them to a new environment.
  • Networking and access. Network configuration, DNS, and permissions that give people and applications the access they need.
  • Deployment automation. Repeatable infrastructure configuration and deployment workflows that make updates easier to manage.

Reliability and ongoing support

We can configure backups, monitoring, and alerts, and help you plan recovery and manage cloud costs. Ongoing maintenance and support are available when included in the agreed scope.

What you receive

A configured environment, documentation of what was set up, and a practical handover covering access, deployment, and maintenance. We agree on the deliverables before work begins, so you know what is included.

How pricing works

Projects start at $300. The final price depends on the environment, workloads, and support required. We confirm the scope and price before work begins. Cloud provider charges are separate from our service fee and depend on the services you use.
