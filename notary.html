Mobile Notary in Lancaster, Ohio

We come to you, at home, at work, or anywhere convenient in the Lancaster area.

Mobile fee from $25 First 10 pages free Lancaster, OH

A notary that travels to you

Getting documents notarised usually means taking time off, driving somewhere, and waiting. We remove that by coming to you anywhere in Lancaster, Ohio, at a time that suits you.

That is especially useful when signing is difficult to arrange: hospital and care home visits, signings with elderly or limited mobility clients, or paperwork that has to be completed outside normal office hours.

How the fees work

There are two separate parts to what you pay, and we keep them clearly itemised.

  • Mobile fee, starting at $25. This is the travel fee for coming to your location. It starts at $25 and depends on distance and timing.
  • Notarial act fees. Your first 10 pages are free. Beyond that, standard Ohio notary fees apply.
Notary fees in Ohio are set by state statute. We charge no more than the statutory maximum for each notarial act, and the travel fee is always shown separately from it.

Documents we commonly notarise

  • Powers of attorney
  • Affidavits and sworn statements
  • Deeds and property paperwork
  • Parental and travel consent forms
  • Vehicle title transfers
  • Loan and financial documents

What to have ready

  • Valid photo ID for every person signing.
  • The complete document, unsigned. Do not sign before we arrive, as we must witness the signature.
  • Any required witnesses, if your document calls for them.

If you are unsure whether your document needs a witness, send us a message and we will tell you before the appointment.
