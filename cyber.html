Cyber Security

Risk assessments and cyber reviews that tell you what is actually exposed, and what to fix first.

Starting at $200 Written findings report

Risk Assessments

A structured look at where your business is exposed, covering the systems you depend on, who has access to them, and what would happen if any of them failed or were breached.

  • Asset and access review. What you run, who can reach it, and whether that access is still appropriate.
  • Threat and impact analysis. What is realistically likely to go wrong, and what it would cost you.
  • Prioritised findings. Issues ranked by risk, so you spend effort where it matters.
  • Remediation plan. Specific next steps, not generic advice.

Cyber Reviews

A focused review of a specific area when you do not need a full assessment. Useful before a launch, after a staff change, or when a client or insurer asks you to demonstrate due diligence.

  • Configuration review. Accounts, permissions, and settings on the services you use.
  • Web application review. Common weaknesses in a site or app before it goes live.
  • Policy and process review. Whether what you have written down matches what actually happens.
  • Third party and vendor review. The risk you inherit from the tools you rely on.

What you receive

Every engagement ends with a written report in plain language: what we looked at, what we found, how serious each finding is, and the recommended fix. It is written so you can hand it to a client, an insurer, or a developer and have it make sense.

We work only within the scope you authorise in writing, and we never test systems you do not own or control.

How pricing works

Engagements start at $200. The final price depends on the number of systems in scope and the depth of review required. We confirm scope and cost in writing before any work begins.
